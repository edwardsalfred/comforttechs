## Patterns of the Top 10%

1. **Persistent sticky CTA bar** — phone + "Request Service" always reachable, on every page, every scroll position. Honeycomb, Cowboys, Champion, Beyer all do this.
2. **Hero offer above the fold** — a tangible price hook ($58 tune-up at Honeycomb, free estimate at Cowboys). Not "Welcome to our company."
3. **Real techs in real photos** — Honeycomb and Cowboys lead with photos of named technicians. Builds trust in 0.5 seconds. Stock imagery looks generic.
4. **Trust stack visible early** — star count, BBB, OEM badges (Trane / Daikin / Amana), local press mentions ("KSAT," "WOAI"). Top performers stack 4–6 trust signals near the hero.
5. **Specific local proof** — "Real Stories from Your San Antonio Neighbors," named neighborhoods, service-area maps. The losers use generic "We service your area."
6. **Two-track CTA** — phone for urgency callers, online form for browsers. Top sites accommodate BOTH.
7. **Founder/family story prominent** — Cowboys leans hard on family ownership, Beyer on "Honor God, Relationships." Comfort Techs has Isidro Ramirez and a Latino family-owned story but currently buries it.
8. **Financing transparency** — partner logos (Synchrony, GoodLeap, Wisetack) and "$X/month" numbers visible on hero. Removes the price objection upfront.

## Recommended design direction (for the rebuild)

**Colors:** Keep the trust-blue HVAC convention, but pair with warm orange CTA. Comfort Techs blue (`#0B3D91`) + bright sky (`#0E72D6`) + orange (`#FF6A2B`). This avoids competitor red/honey territory.

**Typography:** `Inter` body+heading, with `Instrument Serif` for editorial pull quotes (humanizes the brand, sets it apart from competitor sans-only sites).

**Photography:** Lead with REAL photos of Isidro and the team. Replace generic Hibu hero with founder portrait + branded van.

**Animation:** Premium scroll choreography (GSAP + ScrollTrigger). Competitors are 100% static — animated entrance is an instant differentiator at this price tier.

**Sections needed:**
- Hero with founder + trust bar
- Sticky CTA on scroll
- Services grid (animated reveal)
- "Why Comfort Techs" awards row (Amana Newcomer 2024+2025, NATE, TACCA, Daikin)
- Founder story block (Isidro)
- Special offers grid (the $8,888, $89/mo, $29 tune-up)
- Service area list
- Testimonials placeholder
- 24/7 emergency CTA
- Contact + form

**What to AVOID** (competitor weaknesses):
- Cowboys' over-cluttered nav with 7+ submenus
- Beyer's faith-heavy framing (audience-narrowing)
- Best AC's brochure-style 1990s layout
- Champion's confusion of trades (AC + plumbing + electrical) — Comfort Techs should stay HVAC-focused
